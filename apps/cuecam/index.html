Float your script over the Camera app

CueCam turns your script into a small scrolling video that floats on top of the iPhone Camera app (picture-in-picture), so you can film in Cinematic or any other mode.

Text size, scroll speed and mirror come from your settings on the first screen.

Preparing…

  1. Tap Float it. The script pops out into a small window.
  2. Swipe home and open the Camera app. Pick Cinematic (or any mode).
  3. The script waits 6 seconds before it starts scrolling, so you have time to press record.
  4. Drag the window close to the lens and pinch it to make it bigger. Tap it to pause or skip back 15 seconds.

On iPhone, tap Save video and pick Save Video to put it in Photos.
