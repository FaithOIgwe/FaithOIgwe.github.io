Privacy notice and community rules

Outside is in an early test in Lagos and Abuja. This page explains, in plain words, what we keep and why.

What we keep

  • Your email address and password, to sign you in. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider, Supabase, and we never see them.
  • Your first name, city, age range, whether you're a woman (for women-only plans), and the things you want to do.
  • Your WhatsApp number and a trusted contact's name and number.
  • The plans and invitations you join, when you check in and when you say you're home safe.
  • Your private answers about who you'd meet again, your ratings, and any reports you send.

Who can see what

  • Other members see your first name, city and the plans you've joined. They don't see your age, email, number or answers.
  • People you make a plan with can see your WhatsApp number, so you can find each other on the day.
  • The host sees your number and trusted contact, to verify you, help on the day, and follow up if you don't confirm you got home.
  • Your "would you meet again" answers count only when the feeling is mutual. Nobody is told who didn't pick them.

We don't sell your information or show you ads.

Payments

Your first hosted plan has a small fee to hold your spot. Payments go through Paystack or a bank transfer, and we never see your card details.

Deleting your account

Ask the host on WhatsApp to delete your account. Your profile, number, plans and answers are removed with it.

Community rules

  1. Meet only at the public place in the plan. Don't move the group to a private home or a hotel.
  2. Show up, or cancel early. Two no-shows pause your account until the host reviews it.
  3. Be who your profile says you are. Fake profiles are removed.
  4. No dating pressure, selling or recruiting. This is for friendship.
  5. Look out for each other, and report anything that feels off.

You must be 18 or older to use Outside.
