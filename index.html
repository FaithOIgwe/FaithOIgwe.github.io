02
Method
Funnel analytics

An app-to-loan funnel that counts each person once

The trap. Monthly funnels are often built by adding up daily distinct users. Anyone who comes back on a second day gets counted twice, so the monthly numbers run high and can't be compared.

What I do. Compute every stage at the grain being reported, test that the totals tie back to the source, and alert on conversion separately for new and returning customers, so a shift is noticed before the weekly meeting.

SQL · dbt tests · Alerting

05
Method
Metric design

One definition before any dashboard

The trap. "Adoption" of a feature can mean customers who were offered it or customers who actually set it up. When one word covers both, nobody can say cleanly whether the feature is working.

What I do. Write each measure down explicitly, separate new from returning customers, and segment by eligibility, so like is compared with like.

The point. One agreed number to judge the feature by, instead of a debate about whose figure is right.

Same six steps, whether it's a quick question or a migration.

  1. 1 Question

    What decision is this answer for?

  2. 2 Metric

    Write the definition down before any SQL.

  3. 3 Grain

    One row per what? Get it wrong and nothing adds up.

  4. 4 Validate

    Dedupe, tie to a known total, test the edge cases.

  5. 5 Drivers

    Segment until the change has a reason.

  6. 6 Communicate

    Lead with the answer and what to do about it.
